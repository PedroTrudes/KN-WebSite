# Imagens conceituais

As imagens são referências ilustrativas geradas com a ferramenta integrada. Não representam obras executadas pela KN.

## public/assets/arquitetura.webp

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: architectural photograph concept for a premium Brazilian metalwork and glazing website hero. Generate a single photorealistic wide landscape architectural editorial image, 1536x1024 or wider. Subject: elegant contemporary single-story house in a coastal subtropical setting, refined warm off-white plaster walls, graphite black steel-framed floor-to-ceiling glass doors and black steel windows, an understated stone walkway, muted natural greenery at the far edge. Composition: architectural facade primarily on the right two thirds, left third with softly shadowed plain wall and open darker neutral area to leave space for webpage typography; the photograph itself contains NO TEXT. Show the metal and glass construction clearly with realistic straight joinery, plausible building proportions, glass reflections. Mood: quiet sophisticated premium architecture magazine photography, soft late afternoon light, warm grey/cream/charcoal neutral colors. Avoid excessive furniture, saturated colors, people, cars, logos, watermarks, dramatic fake golden gradients. This is an illustrative design reference, not an actual company's project.

## public/assets/catalogo.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: illustrated photo reference catalog sheet for a premium metalwork and glazing landing page. Create ONE landscape image with a PERFECT 2x2 grid of four equally sized photographic panels, overall 4:3 ratio, no spacing, no borders, no text or watermarks. Each panel is a different high-end architectural editorial photo reference in a cohesive restrained cream/grey/charcoal palette. TOP LEFT: an elegant closed black steel-framed hinged entrance door with large glass panes set in an off-white facade, refined realistic hardware. TOP RIGHT: a rectangular black steel-framed window with straight mullions in a pale plaster wall, warm light through glass. BOTTOM LEFT: elegant clear glass entrance doors with discreet graphite metal frame, realistic handles, sophisticated minimal interior beyond. BOTTOM RIGHT: an elegant closed graphite iron gate with slim vertical bars in a warm stone/plaster architectural wall, close view that highlights refined metal craftsmanship. Every panel must have straight plausible lines, show the entire relevant door/window/gate without cutting off its edges, clean architectural detail, neutral natural light, no humans, no signage, no brands. Avoid adding images beyond these exactly four panels. All images are illustrative design references, not actual company projects.

As saídas PNG foram convertidas em WebP para compressão, sem alterações visuais ou recortes. Os recortes de referência são apresentados por CSS a partir dos quatro quadrantes.
